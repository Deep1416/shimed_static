Thioshine A

Relaxation wiTHout Sedation

₹245 MRP per strip of 10 tablets

Thioshine A combines Aceclofenac 100 mg and Thiocolchicoside 4 mg to treat muscle spasms and pain together in a single tablet. Aceclofenac, one of the most popular analgesic and anti-inflammatory drugs, works with Thiocolchicoside — a potent muscle relaxant — to restore mobility and relieve back pain without causing sedation.

Composition

Aceclofenac 100 mg & Thiocolchicoside 4 mg Tablets

Indications

  • Sprain and Strain
  • Sport Injuries
  • Skeletal Muscle Spasm
  • Acute Pain Associated with Muscle Spasms
  • Spondylosis
  • Rheumatoid Arthritis
  • Osteoarthritis

Key Benefits

  • Dual action: analgesic + muscle relaxant in one tablet
  • Relaxes Back Pain and Restores Mobility
  • No sedation — you stay alert while pain is relieved
  • Effective for both acute and chronic musculoskeletal conditions
